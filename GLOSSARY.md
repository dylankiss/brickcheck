# BrickCheck

Checking your LEGO sets against a pile of loose pieces, part by part, alone or together, to find out what's missing.

## Language

### Sets

**Set**:
One physical LEGO set you are checking, such as your copy of Little House. Two copies of the same Catalog set are two Sets. "Check" is what you do to a Set, never a thing.
_Avoid_: Check (as a noun), copy, session, project, hunt

**Catalog set**:
A LEGO product as it is listed in the catalog, identified by its set number, such as 40516-1. A Set is always of one Catalog set.
_Avoid_: Kit, model, product

### Parts

**Part**:
One shape in one color, such as a red 2x4 brick. Its part number names the shape and is shared by every color.
_Avoid_: Element, tile, item, brick, design, mould

**Piece**:
One physical object in your hand. A Part needs some number of Pieces; "piece" is only used for counting.
_Avoid_: Brick, unit

**Spare**:
An additional Piece of a Part that LEGO puts in the box on top of what the Catalog set needs.
_Avoid_: Extra

**Minifigure part**:
A Part that belongs to one of the Catalog set's minifigures rather than to the build itself.
_Avoid_: Fig part, minifig piece

### People

**Owner**:
A person who manages a Set: the one who added it, or an Owner of the Group it is in. A Group can have several Owners, and a Group's Owner is an Owner of every Set in it. They can do everything a Helper can, and only Owners can invite people, change roles, remove members, reset counts or delete the Set. A Group always keeps at least one Owner.
_Avoid_: Admin, creator

**Helper**:
A member of a Set, or of its Group, who can tick off parts and rename the Set. A Group role applies to every Set in the Group; when someone has both, the higher role wins.
_Avoid_: Editor, collaborator, contributor

**Viewer**:
A member of a Set who can follow progress but not tick off parts.
_Avoid_: Spectator, guest, read-only member

**Invite**:
A link the Owner shares to let someone join a Set as a Helper or a Viewer, until it expires or the Owner revokes it. It never changes the role of someone who is already a member.
_Avoid_: Share link, join link

**Group**:
A named set of people who share access to the Sets in it. A person can be in several Groups; a Set is in at most one.
_Avoid_: Household, team, family, workspace

**Group invite**:
A link a Group Owner shares to let someone join a Group, until it expires or is revoked. It never changes the role of someone who is already a member. Not an Invite, which is for one Set.
_Avoid_: Share link, join link

### Inventory and progress

**Inventory line**:
One line of a Catalog set's inventory: a part, its quantity, whether it's a spare, and the minifigure it belongs to, if any. The same part can appear on several lines.
_Avoid_: Row, set part

**Found**:
How many Pieces of a Part someone has ticked off in a Set. "Tick off" is the action; found is the state.
_Avoid_: Checked, ticked, collected

**Missing**:
Needed minus found, counted only over the inventory lines the Set includes. A spare isn't missing in a Set that leaves spares out.
_Avoid_: Lacking, lost, remaining

**Complete**:
A Set with nothing missing. It's a fact, not something anyone declares.
_Avoid_: Done, finished, closed
